The Noise of Traditional Social Media

Modern algorithms are intentionally engineered to overstimulate. They capitalize on infinite scrolls, blinky notification badges, and hyper-saturated visuals designed to hook attention by inducing subconscious anxiety.

For neurodivergent individuals, this creates sensory overload, intense mental fatigue, and social anxiety. Traditional feeds make it incredibly hard to filter noise and connect safely without feeling overwhelmed.

How NeuroComet is Re-wired for You

Custom Sensory Controls

Tailor your UI to match your mind. Toggle reduced motion, contrast profiles, adjustable font families, and soft sensory presets (Calm, Focus, Energy).

Strict Safety & Verification

Interact in a community free from harassment. Benefit from robust content filter-guardrails, muted word blockers, parental support links, and optional verification.

Meaningful Micro-Communities

Connect on your own terms. Find your orbit through focused micro-communities with zero algorithmic pressure, no infinite scrolls, and no toxic metrics.

State-of-the-Art Security

Your account and data are protected by the latest authentication standards. We believe peace of mind is essential for a safe social space.

Biometric Login

Use your fingerprint or face scan to securely log in without remembering passwords.

Passkey Support

Next-generation phishing-resistant FIDO2 hardware keys and passkeys supported natively.

Authenticator Apps (2FA)

Link your favorite authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.) for robust two-factor security.

Backup Codes

Never lose access to your account with secure, one-time-use backup codes.
